## Notes

- Regenerate: `make -C sim PDK=<PDK> report` for each PDK, then `make -C sim compare` (writes
  `sim/outputs/compare.md`; this file is a copy of it). Each report is
  `sim/outputs/<PDK>/report/pll_design.html`.
- ics55 keeps its hand-tuned reference design (fixed in `lib/ics55.mk`); the automatic method chooses the
  same point (1 GHz, 4096/256). The other PDKs use the automatic target of `src/pll_target.py`.
- `ihp-sg13cmos5l` is electrically identical to `ihp-sg13g2` before layout (same cell netlists and model
  files; every characterization agrees to 6 digits), so it gives the same numbers. Its ISF result is
  reused from sg13g2 (`ISF_FROM_PDK` in `lib/ihp-sg13cmos5l.mk`); its other simulations ran on its own netlists.
- DCO noise of the 130 nm PDKs: ISF with 64 phases, an injection every 2 periods and a 5 ps maximum step
  (about 4x faster; edge errors 0.1-27 fs against shifts of ~40-260 fs). ics55 also has a Spectre
  PSS+pnoise cross-check (within 1 dB).
- RTL TIE is noise-free (delay models only), measured after the first 20 % of the record after LOCKED
  (the gains after the gear shift finish the pull-in). With injection the behavioral model is optimistic
  at N = 4 (sg13g2: RTL 1.27 ps vs model 0.38 ps): it treats the edge replacement as ideal.
- Gaps of the tuning curve (coverage < 1): sky130 fine range = 0.96 of the largest mid step, sg13g2 mid
  range = 0.99 of the largest coarse step. Small, but a real design would add fine/mid range.
- Pull-in limits the 130 nm designs: a bang-bang loop pulls in with its proportional step, and the sg13g2
  fine line saturates at the top (code 31 is faster than code 30). Higher N needs a larger K_P than the
  beta_p <= 1 % T rule allows.
- Not done: place and route of the new PDKs (`pnr/` is ics55-specific), STA of `pll_logic` at f_OUT/2 in the
  130 nm PDKs, full transistor-level PLL simulation.
